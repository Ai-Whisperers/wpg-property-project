# Stakeholders

## Our Side

### John & Sonia
- **Role:** Property owners
- **Own:** 7 adjacent parcels (4 one side, 3 other side), street to street
- **Location:** End of street, near Monravía (between San Lorenzo and Asunción)
- **Want:** Exchange back property for neighbor's property (to expand compound)
- **Don't want:** Just money - no monetary incentive

### Grace
- **Relationship:** Sonia's closest friend
- **Offer:** Can build house on the back property
- **Preference:** Would rather have her on the compound (next door)
- **Impact:** If she builds → exchange opportunity closes permanently

### Gerold
- **Relationship:** John's old friend, former Sys admin at WPG
- **History:** Already did property exchange with John & Sonia
- **Current location:** Lives next to John's house
