# Negotiation Strategies

## Understanding the Blocker

The wife doesn't want to sell. But WHY?

### Possible Reasons

1. **Emotional attachment** - Memories, BBQ gatherings, "our place"
2. **Fear of change** - Known vs unknown
3. **Distrust** - Doesn't trust the deal/buyers
4. **Lack of vision** - Can't imagine something better
5. **Relationship dynamics** - Doesn't want husband to "win"
6. **Hidden value** - Property means something we don't know about
7. **No compelling alternative** - "What would we do instead?"

## Strategy Options

### 1. Discovery First
**Approach:** Find out what she actually wants
- Direct conversation: "If you could have anything, what would it be?"
- Listen for hidden desires
- Don't propose solutions until you understand

### 2. Trade-Up Offer
**Approach:** Offer something better than what she has
- A better BBQ location?
- A vacation property?
- Membership to a club?
- Something she always wanted but never asked for?

### 3. Create Vision
**Approach:** Help her see what's possible
- Show examples of what others have done
- Paint a picture of the alternative life
- Make the abstract concrete

### 4. Address the Fear
**Approach:** Remove the uncertainty
- Guaranteed transition
- Keep some connection to the area
- Trial period?

### 5. Social Proof
**Approach:** Leverage her network
- Does she have friends who would validate the move?
- Can Sonia or Grace connect with her?
- Women-to-women conversation might work better

### 6. Time-Limited Opportunity
**Approach:** Create urgency (carefully)
- The football player won't wait forever
- Grace is about to build
- Frame it as "this door is closing"
- ⚠️ Risk: Pressure can backfire

### 7. The Dream Question
**Approach:** Open-ended exploration
> "If money and logistics weren't a problem, what would your ideal situation look like?"

Most people have never been asked this. The answer might surprise you.

## Key Principles

1. **Don't negotiate against yourself** - Let her tell you what she wants
2. **The husband is already sold** - Work on the wife directly
3. **Sonia's pessimism might be useful** - Low expectations = no pressure
4. **Football player is an ally** - Coordinate with him
5. **Grace timeline is leverage** - But use carefully
